# JacHacks SF 2026 Project Rubric

**4 criteria / 100%**

- **Date:** July 26, 2026
- **Venue:** Founders Inc
- **Demo time:** 4 minutes
- **Format:** Parallel groups

This is exactly what you're scored on. Each criterion is scored **1-5** from your 4-minute demo, then weighted. **Use of Jac is worth double any other criterion.**

## Required to Win

**You must meaningfully use Jac to qualify for any track or any prize.** That means scoring at least a **3** on Use of Jac.

## 1. Use of Jac - 40%

*Is Jac doing real work at the center of your project?*

| Score | Rating | Description |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Peripheral | Jac is bolted on, boilerplate, or barely touched. The product would work the same without it. **Not eligible for prizes.** |
| 3 | Meaningful | Real logic lives in Jac and drives part of your main workflow, not just a thin wrapper around another stack. **Minimum to qualify.** |
| 5 | Central | The product depends on Jac, used with depth or originality: walkers, graph traversal, byLLM, agentic flows. |

> **Be ready to show where Jac runs.** Point to it in your repo or your demo, don't just say it. The highest score here also wins the **Best JacHammer** award.

## 2. Real-World Use Case - 20%

*Would someone outside this room actually use it?*

| Score | Rating | Description |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Unclear | Hard to name the user or the problem being solved. |
| 3 | Clear | Real user, real problem, credible value. |
| 5 | Compelling | A problem worth solving, and a convincing way to solve it. |

> Name one person, not a market. A narrow, focused problem can earn a 5.

## 3. Technical Execution - 20%

*How much did you build, and how well does it hold up?*

| Score | Rating | Description |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Thin | Mostly scaffolding, templates, or hand-waving. |
| 3 | Solid | A real build with the hard part genuinely done. |
| 5 | Impressive | Ambitious scope, cleanly pulled off in one day. |

> You're judged against one day of building, not a funded roadmap.

## 4. Demo & Story - 20%

*Did you show the core product working, end to end?*

| Score | Rating | Description |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Incomplete | Slides, mockups, or a workflow that never runs. |
| 3 | Functional | Main flow runs start to finish, rough edges and all. |
| 5 | Convincing | Complete, clear, and demoed with real evidence. |

> A working demo beats a deck. You're scored on what you show, not what you promise.

## In Your 4 Minutes

1. Say who it's for, and what breaks for them today.
2. Run the core workflow live. Don't describe it.
3. Show where Jac runs inside your product.
